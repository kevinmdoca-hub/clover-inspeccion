Clover Inspection PWA v0.7.8

Focused reliability patch on the approved v0.7.5 baseline.

Fixes:
- PDF generation crash corrected. The report header was calling the embedded-logo image routine with a missing image ID, which caused PDF generation to fail whenever the logo loaded successfully.
- PDF image placement is now guarded against invalid dimensions/arguments.
- Manual section changes no longer rely on the browser's native <details> scroll behavior. The tapped section stays visually anchored while the previously open section collapses.
- Continue and Missing-item navigation still deliberately move the target section to the top.

Preserved:
- Approved 5-column condition report: ID | ITEM | STATUS | COMMENTS / NOTES | EVIDENCE.
- ID/Item/Status/Evidence vertical centering; Status/Evidence horizontal centering.
- 3-column standardized photo page, abnormal evidence mapping, video-reference-only behavior, service-planning summary, bilingual PDF language from Settings, offline saving, evidence persistence, and one-unit/one-section accordion rules.

Market/model catalog enrichment remains a separate patch.


v0.7.8 patch:
- Section 1 completion no longer double-counts the required data-plate photo.
- Abnormal-finding evidence videos use a 10-second maximum and 50 MB maximum per clip; each finding allows up to 3 total evidence files.
- Section 13 general functional-test videos retain a 30-second maximum and 50 MB maximum per clip, with up to 3 functional-test videos per unit.
- Removed video-camera emoji from controls/previews.
- 'No observado / Not observed' status control no longer breaks a word mid-label on mobile.
